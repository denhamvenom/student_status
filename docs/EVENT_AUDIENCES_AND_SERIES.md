# Event audiences, Mentor invitations and recurring meetings

November 2026 sprint. This describes implemented source behavior. Production release and signed-in production checks are recorded separately in `docs/releases/`.

## Subteams

- **Primary subteam** (`students.subteam`): Build, Electrical, Machining, Programming, Business/Media (`src/lib/subteams.ts`). Legacy names are read as current ones: Mechanical → Build; Business, Outreach, Business and Media → Business/Media.
- **Secondary subteam** (`students.secondarySubteam`): only `CAD`, or none.
- **Leadership** (`students.leadership`): a yes/no flag. It behaves as an audience group.
- **Who can set them:** a Coach or a non-parent Travel Lead can change a student's secondary subteam and Leadership (`studentSubteams.setStudentSecondaryAndLeadership`, revision-checked and audited). The change also adds or removes the student from future required subteam events they now match (`syncSubteamMembership`).
- **Mentor subteams** (`appUsers.subteams`): the primary subteams plus CAD, but not Leadership. **Only a Coach** can set them (`setMentorSubteams`, edited on `/adults`), for a Mentor account or a Parent with Mentor staff access. They are used to invite Mentors by subteam and to decide which subteam-only events a Mentor may sign up for.

A student matches a subteam audience through their primary subteam (canonicalized), their secondary subteam, or Leadership when Leadership is chosen (`convex/lib/eventAudienceRules.ts`).

## Who an event is for

The wizard asks "Who is this event for?" and then "Is this event required?":

| Audience | Required (Yes) | Optional (No) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone (all active students, not Mentors) | `signupMode: assigned`, `assignmentScope: wholeTeam` | `signupMode: open` (or `approval`), no scope |
| Subteams (at least one group) | `assigned`, `selected`; every matching student is enrolled by a job | `open`/`approval`, no scope; only matching students see it |
| Specific students (at least one, max 200) | `assigned`, `selected`; the listed students are assigned | `open`/`approval`; only listed students see it |
| Mentors only (at least one Mentor invite) | Not allowed | `signupMode: assigned`, no scope, no students |

`toStoredEventFields` stores `audienceKind`, `audienceSubteams` and `mandatory` together with the mapped `signupMode`/`assignmentScope`. Listed students are stored in `eventAudienceStudents`. When `audienceKind` is set, callers cannot also pass a scope or `assignedStudentIds`. Required subteam events and Whole Team events cannot have a capacity.

**Legacy events** (saved before audiences existed, no `audienceKind`) are read by `resolveAudience` like this:
- `assigned` + `wholeTeam` → Everyone, required
- `assigned` + `selected`, `travelTeam` or no scope → specific students, required
- `open`/`approval` → Everyone, optional

Their stored fields are not rewritten.

## Mentor invitations

- At creation, Coaches and Travel Leads can invite Mentors one at a time (max 200). For a Subteams audience they can also invite **Mentors of those subteams** (`inviteSubteamMentors`). Invite rows (`eventMentorInvites`) record their source as `individual` or `subteam`. Inviting by subteam reads each account list (Mentor accounts, Parents with Mentor access, Parents with Travel Lead access) up to 500; if any list is longer, the invite is refused with a message to invite Mentors individually (never silently cut short).
- After creation, `mentorInvites.addMentorInvites` / `removeMentorInvite` and `forEvent` / `forEventSummary` (which include Mentors' sign-up answers and counts) are available to a Coach or any Travel Lead, **including a Parent acting as Travel Lead**, on any current event in the active season. Owner decision, Nov 6 2026 (supersedes the earlier "non-parent Travel Lead only" rule for sign-ups and answers): Parents with a Mentor or Travel Lead grant are Mentors first, Parents second. Invitees may be Mentor accounts or Parents with Mentor or Travel Lead staff access. Only newly added Mentors are notified.
- **Event page Mentors section (owner decision, Nov 7 2026):** on the Coach / Travel Lead event page the Mentors list with answers is its own **Mentors** section (`EventMentorsSection`), no longer inside the collapsed "Who it's for". Its header line always shows the totals, for example "Mentors · 3 going · 1 waiting · 2 can drive · 1 hotel room" (waiting only when someone hasn't answered; drive and hotel only when the event asks those questions). It starts open when at least one Mentor is going or waiting, and the invite controls sit in it while the event is open.
- **Created by (owner decision, Nov 7 2026):** for a Coach or Travel Lead only, `app.listEvents` rows and `app.eventDetail` add `createdBy` (`lib/eventCreator.ts`): `{ name, roleLabel: "Coach" | "Travel Lead" | "Mentor", mentorMeeting }` or null when the creator is unknown (the event's own `createdBy`, else its series' creator). A Parent with staff access is labeled by their grant. `mentorMeeting` is true when a Mentor (not a Coach or Travel Lead) created it and it is still a Mentor meeting (`isMentorMeetingNow`). Other roles get no `createdBy`. The event page shows "Created by <name> (Mentor)"; the Events list tags Mentor meetings "Mentor meeting".
- **Mentor directory:** `studentSubteams.listMentorsForInvite` (id, name and subteams only; no email, phone or other account data) may be read by a Coach, a Travel Lead and **any Mentor**, including a Parent with a Mentor or Travel Lead grant, so they can invite Mentors to their own meetings. Each account list is read up to 500; a longer list is refused with a clear message instead of being cut short.
- Mentors see their own invitations at **`/invited`** (`MentorInvitedEvents`, `mentorInvites.myInvitedEvents`). They see only title, time, place, virtual flag, cancelled flag and their own status: no rosters, fees or health data. A Mentor answers only their own invitation (accepted/declined). A declined invitation for a current event is still listed, so the Mentor can accept later. Canceled, archived, ended and other-season events refuse answers. Declining a `self` sign-up through `respond` deletes the row, the same as Withdraw.

## Location type: Virtual (Discord)

`locationType` is `inPerson` (default) or `virtual`, labeled **Virtual (Discord)**. A virtual event cannot also be on-site. The server forces `permissionRequired: false`, `overnight: false`, `roomingEnabled: false`, no room deadline and `travel: "None"`, and the wizard skips those steps. Whether virtual events should ever need a permission form is still an owner decision (see KNOWN_GAPS).

## Team Calendar choice

New events offer **Create a separate Calendar entry**, **Connect an existing Calendar event**, or **Don't add to Team Calendar** (`src/features/event-setup/new-event-defaults.ts`). Since November 7, 2026, every one-time event defaults to Create, Team meetings included. The exception is a one-time Team meeting on a Required weekday (Tuesday before kickoff, Monday from kickoff), which starts off Team Calendar so attendance doesn't count it as Required. Repeating meetings default to **Don't add to Team Calendar**. Mentors get a simpler **Add to Team Calendar** checkbox for one-time meetings only (see [EVENT_CALENDAR_SYNC.md](EVENT_CALENDAR_SYNC.md)). A repeating meeting can get its own Team Calendar series (`addToCalendar`) or stay off it. It cannot connect to a single existing entry. To manage an existing Google series, a Coach adopts it (see below).

**+ Team meeting** (Coach and Travel Lead, `TeamMeetingSheet`): optional by default (owner decision, Nov 7 2026); a Coach can still choose Required, a Travel Lead can't require a group.

## Recurring meetings (`eventSeries`)

- **Rule:** weekly in America/Chicago: weekdays, every N weeks, a start time, a length, and optionally an end date or a number of meetings. The series must start today or later. The rule is anchored on its first meeting.
- **Rolling window:** each meeting is an ordinary `events` row (`seriesId`, `seriesOccurrenceKey` = Chicago date). Rows are generated **56 days (8 weeks)** ahead in small batches. A **daily cron** ("extend recurring meetings", 10:15 UTC, `eventSeries.tick`) extends each active series, ends a series that has no meetings left, and reconciles series that are on Team Calendar.
- **This meeting only** (`editOccurrence`, `cancelOccurrence`): uses the single-event edit path. The meeting becomes a detached override (`seriesDetached`). On Calendar it becomes an instance exception.
- **This and following** (`editFuture`, `cancelFuture`): always a **split**. Meetings from the chosen date move to a new series (same rows and keys; detached overrides keep their changes). Meetings that no longer fit the new rule are cancelled, and the old series ends the day before. The chosen meeting must be after today, and no Timeclock attendance may exist on or after it.
- **Notifications:** creating a series sends **one** notice (for the first meeting, to the audience students and invited Mentors). Generated meetings and enrollment are silent. This-and-following and cancel-following send one representative notice (the first affected meeting). Adopting a Google series sends nothing.
- **Duplicate guard:** a new series is refused if it overlaps a series already on Team Calendar here (same start time, a shared weekday, overlapping dates), such as the adopted Tuesday meeting.

## Permissions: Coach vs Travel Lead

| Action | Coach | Travel Lead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Optional events, any audience; required specific students | Yes | Yes |
| Required Everyone (Whole Team) or required Subteams | Yes | No |
| Create or edit recurring series | Yes | Yes, except required Everyone/Subteams series |
| Adopt an existing Team Calendar series | Yes | No |
| Set a student's secondary subteam or Leadership | Yes | Yes (non-parent Travel Lead) |
| Set Mentor subteams | Yes | No |
| Invite or remove Mentors on an event | Yes | Yes, including Coach-only required events (current policy, see KNOWN_GAPS) |

Mentors have a separate, narrower set of powers over **their own** Team meetings (see below). "Mentor" here means every Mentor account (with or without Travel Lead) **and every Parent with Mentor or Travel Lead staff access** (`isMentorCapable` in `convex/lib/mentorMeetings.ts`, owner decision Nov 6 2026): such a Parent counts as a Mentor first for these features, whatever role their session selected, acting as a plain Mentor (no Travel Lead powers here); their Parent data access does not widen. The server enforces all of it; hiding a control is not authorization.

| Action | Mentor (not Travel Lead) |
| --- | --- |
| Create a one-time Team meeting (`mentorMeetings.createMeeting`) or repeating series (`eventSeries.create`) | Yes, optional only: Everyone (open signup), Subteams, specific students, or Mentors only |
| Invite other Mentors to a meeting they create | Yes (individually, or by subteam for a Subteams audience) |
| Required meetings (Whole Team, required subteams or students), fees, travel, overnight, rooms, permission forms, FIRST requirement, Outreach flag, approval signup, Team Calendar, non-Team types | No (refused on the server) |
| Edit or cancel a meeting or series | Only ones they created, within the same limits: this meeting only (`editMeeting`, `cancelMeeting`, `editOccurrence`, `cancelOccurrence`), this and following (`editFuture`, `cancelFuture`) and end date (`setEndDate`) |
| Sign up for an event open to them, answer its questions, and withdraw | Yes (`mentorMeetings.signUp`, `updateAnswers`, `withdraw` / `skipMeeting`, `leaveSeries`; `mentorInvites.respond` for invitations) |
| Read an event's minimized projection | Created, invited/accepted, or eligible to sign up (`mentorMeetings.view`) |
| Anything on an event a Coach or Travel Lead created | Sign up / respond / read only; no changes |

## Mentor meetings and self sign-up

- **Creator:** `events.createdBy` (every event created through `admin.createEvent` or `mentorMeetings.createMeeting`) and `eventSeries.createdBy`. A meeting generated from a series belongs to its series' creator. Creator rights (manage, full roster in `view`, the created list on Home, M5 notices) count only while the event is still a Mentor meeting: it passes `mentorMeetingLimitError` and is not linked to Team Calendar (`mentorMeetingCreatorId`). So a Travel Lead who created a travel or fee Competition has no Mentor-creator rights on it, including after losing Travel Lead. The creator counts as **going** only on the same terms: while `mentorMeetingCreatorId` says the event is their Mentor meeting, `listMeetingMentors` lists them as accepted without a row (M1 schedule notices, M2 reminders). The creator of any other event (for example a Travel Lead's overnight Competition) is treated like anyone else: they sign up through the normal wizard (answering the drive/hotel questions), it appears in their joinable list on Home, they count in Coach totals only through their own sign-up/invite row, and they get M1/M2 only when signed up. A series a Mentor created stays theirs when a Coach or Travel Lead later changes it with "this and following".
- **Length:** a Mentor meeting lasts at most 12 hours (`createMeeting`, time edits in `editMeeting`, and the series rule's 720-minute limit).
- **Who can sign up (owner decision, Nov 6 2026):** a Mentor sees and joins **any** upcoming (not ended) current-season event that is not canceled or archived, of any type (Team meetings, competitions, travel, overnight, outreach, fundraisers), **except** an event whose audience is specific subteams that include none of the Mentor's subteams (`appUsers.subteams`, legacy names canonicalized). Everyone, Travel Team, specific students, Mentors only, required and legacy events are all open; fees, travel, overnight, rooming and permission forms no longer make an event invite-only. One helper (`selfSignupEligible`) drives self sign-up, the joinable list, who may open an event (`view`) and the M4 notice. An invitation always lets the invited Mentor answer.
- **Sign-up answers:** when a Mentor signs up or accepts an invitation they answer only `canDriveStudents` (required when the event involves travel: `travel` is not `None`, and the event is not on site or virtual) and `needsHotelRoom` (required when the event is overnight); otherwise nothing is asked (`mentorQuestions`, `requireAnswers`). A missing required answer is refused on the server; answers to questions the event doesn't ask are dropped. Answers are stored on the Mentor's `eventMentorInvites` row (`canDriveStudents`, `needsHotelRoom`, `answeredAt`) and can be changed while the event is upcoming (`mentorMeetings.updateAnswers`, optionally for every upcoming meeting of the series with `allMeetingsInSeries`). A Mentor sees only their own answers (`view` / Home rows: `questions`, `myAnswers`); Coaches and Travel Leads (including a Parent acting as Travel Lead, owner decision Nov 6 2026) see everyone's with counts (`mentorInvites.forEvent`, `forEventSummary`: accepted, invited, declined, drivers, hotel rooms, unanswered).
- **Row:** signing up inserts an `eventMentorInvites` row `{ source: "self", status: "accepted" }`. An already invited Mentor who signs up keeps their invite source (status becomes accepted). Withdraw deletes a `self` row, and sets an invited row back to `declined`. Canceled, archived and ended events refuse sign-up and withdraw; an event holds at most 200 Mentor rows. The creator of a Mentor meeting is already on it (listed as accepted without a row), so they can't also self-sign-up; a Coach invite to the creator is still answered normally. The creator of any other event signs up like anyone else (see **Creator**).
- **Repeating series sign-up** (`mentorSeriesSignups`, one row per series and Mentor): signing up for (or accepting an invitation to) a meeting of a series takes a `scope`: `all` (every current and future meeting, including ones generated later), `one` (just this meeting; the default) or `next` with `count` 1–52 (this meeting and the following non-canceled meetings the Mentor hasn't skipped, up to that number, including ones generated later, then it stops; skipped meetings don't count toward the number when it is chosen and stay skipped). The row stores `scope`, `count`, `coveredKeys` (the meetings a one/next sign-up covers), `skippedKeys`, the answers (applied to every row the sign-up creates) and `status` (`active`/`left`); rows it creates or accepts carry `seriesSignupId`. Applying a sign-up touches the series' upcoming meetings in one transaction (bounded to 200; the 8-week window holds far fewer). Generation (`lib/seriesGenerate.createOccurrence` → `applySeriesSignups`) puts `all` sign-ups, and `next` sign-ups not yet at their number, on each new meeting, never on a skipped date. `one` added to an existing sign-up keeps its choice; choosing `all` or `next` again replaces the choice but doesn't take the Mentor off meetings already on their schedule.
  - **Skip this meeting** (`skipMeeting`, the same as `withdraw` and declining through `respond`): removes that one row and records the date in `skippedKeys`; the sign-up stays and never puts the Mentor back on that date. Skipping a meeting a `next` sign-up already covers still counts it toward the number (the sign-up isn't extended). Choosing `all` or `next` again (from any other meeting) never clears a skip: a skipped meeting is rejoined only by signing up for (or accepting) that meeting itself, which un-skips it. Example: after skipping meeting 3, changing to "next 4" from meeting 1 covers meetings 1, 2, 4 and 5, and meeting 3 stays skipped.
  - **Leave all** (`leaveSeries`): ends the sign-up and takes the Mentor off every upcoming meeting of the series (self rows deleted, invited rows declined).
  - **Split** ("this and following", `editFuture`): every active sign-up continues on the new series before it generates meetings: `all` stays `all`; `next` keeps the number it still had to go (its number minus meetings before the split); `one` keeps its chosen meetings from the split on; skips on or after the split move too; moved rows point at the new sign-up. The old sign-up keeps what came before (an `all` one ends there).
  - **Display:** `view` and Home rows include `seriesSignup` (`{ scope, count, coversThisMeeting, skippedThisMeeting }` or null) so the UI can say "Every meeting", "Next 4 meetings" or "Just this one".
  - **Notifications:** one M5 "Mentor signed up" for the sign-up (not one per meeting), one M5 "dropped out" for Leave all; M2 reminders stay per meeting attended.
- **Minimized read** (`mentorMeetings.view`, rows in `mentorHome`): title, type, time, place, virtual flag, canceled flag, audience summary, the sign-up questions with the Mentor's own answers, their series sign-up, signed-up Mentors' names (never other Mentors' answers), and for events the Mentor created or accepted only: confirmed students' display names and subteams. No fees, balances, health, permission forms, Parent contacts, emails or phone numbers.
- **Client time (`asOf`):** `view` and `mentorHome` take the client's time because Convex queries must not read the clock. The server can't check it against the real time inside a query, so it never widens access (`plausibleAsOf`): it must be finite, is rounded down to the minute, and is raised to the latest time the server knows has passed (the creation time of the event, season or account it read). Eligibility also requires the event not to be archived, and the server archives every event when it ends (`eventArchive.atEnd` at `endsAt`, plus the one-minute sweep): time state materialized by scheduled mutations. So an old or zero `asOf` can't open a past event the Mentor wasn't part of (the Mentor-meeting creator and invited/signed-up Mentors still can), and a far-future `asOf` only hides events. Mutations re-check everything with `Date.now()`.
- **Home:** `mentorHome.mentorHome` returns the Mentor's scheduled (accepted or self) meetings, pending invites, meetings open for sign-up, meetings they created, their repeating series, their subteams and `canCreateMeeting: true`. Reads are indexed and bounded: the Mentor's own invite rows (`eventMentorInvites.by_mentorUserId_and_status`, 500 per status), meetings they created (`events.by_createdBy_and_endsAt`, plus their series' upcoming meetings), and a capped scan (600) of the season's upcoming events for meetings to join. Ended series are skipped; at most 50 series per status, 30 meetings per series and 300 series meetings in total are read. Every list (scheduled, pending invites, joinable, created) holds up to 100 rows; `truncated` is true when any read cap or list cap was hit, and Your schedule then shows "Showing the next 100. Ask a Coach if you can't find a meeting." Home shows five of each with **See all** links to `/invited#join` and `/invited#created` (Your schedule), which list the full lists with the same Sign up / Leave actions.
  - **Events you can join, grouped (owner decision, Nov 7 2026):** `joinable` lists **one entry per repeating series** (not one per week) and one per one-time event, built from the same eligible meetings as `signUpAvailable` (which stays per meeting), soonest next meeting first, up to 100 entries. Each entry has `kind` (`series`/`event`), `title` (the series name), `seriesId`, `repeatLabel`, `scheduleLabel` (the series rule in America/Chicago, for example "Tuesdays and Thursdays at 6:00 PM"; null for a one-time event), `nextEventId`/`nextStartsAt` and `next` (the soonest meeting the Mentor can still join, as a full row; the sign-up wizard opens on it), `upcomingCount` (upcoming non-canceled meetings of the series in the scan, whether or not the Mentor is on them), `joinableCount` (the ones they can still join) and `hasSeriesSignup` (an active series sign-up such as "Just this one" or "Next 4").
  - **Groups:** each entry has a `category` (`joinCategory`; owner decision, Nov 7 2026, revised the same day): `competitionsAndTrips` only for a Competition and for an overnight trip; `meetings` (shown as **Meetings & events**) otherwise, so Fundraisers, Outreach and Team meetings stay there even when travel is set. Competitions and trips are for the whole team, not a subteam: `forYourSubteam` is true only for a meeting whose audience is restricted to subteams that include one of the Mentor's subteams (`forMentorSubteam`), and always false for `competitionsAndTrips`.
- **Who on my subteams is clocked in** (`mentorClockedIn.mySubteamClockedIn`, Mentor Home): Mentors only (`isMentorCapable`: a Mentor account or a Parent with a current Mentor or Travel Lead grant); Students, Parents without a grant (including a removed grant) and Coaches are refused. For each of the Mentor's subteams (in the order a Coach set them; primary subteams and CAD, legacy names canonicalized) it returns the active, current-season students of that subteam who have an **open** Timeclock clock-in right now: `displayName` (preferred name, else legal name), `subteam` (the student's primary subteam) and `clockedInAt`, plus a `count` per subteam and a `totalCount` of distinct students (a Build + CAD student is listed under both and counted once). A student matches through their primary or secondary (CAD) subteam. No student ids, contact, health or finance data. Reads are indexed and bounded: open sessions of the active season (`timeclockSessions.by_seasonId_and_state`, up to 20) and their clock-ins with no clock-out (`timeclockIntervals.by_sessionId_and_outAt`, up to 500 in total); `truncated` is true when a cap was hit. It takes the client's `asOf` (plausibleAsOf); an open session already past its effective end counts as closed.
- **M4 recipients:** every Mentor who may sign up for the new event under the rule above (**every event type**), except its creator, the account creating it and Mentors already invited. `queueMentorMeetingOpenDigest` pages through Mentor accounts by the `by_role` index (200 per transaction; a scheduled page size must be finite and is clamped to 1–500) and schedules `mentorMeetings.continueOpenDigest` for the next page; the last page also covers Parents with Mentor or Travel Lead staff access (`by_parentStaffAccess`): up to 200 per grant in the same transaction, and a longer grant list is paged in its own scheduled phase (`phase: "parentMentor"` / `"parentTravelLead"`), so nobody is silently left out. Queued for the 5 PM summary.
- **M5 student activity:** the meeting's Mentor creator hears when a student signs up (`participation.signUp`: Signed up, or Pending as "Signup to approve"), when a Signed up student leaves through a withdrawal (`withdrawals.requestWithdrawal` when it takes effect at once, or `finalizeWithdrawal` after Coach/Travel Lead approval), and when a student comes back (`withdrawals.putBackOnEvent`, or `withdrawals.undoWithdrawal` to Signed up; an Undo to Pending is "Signup to approve"). Waitlisted students joining or leaving are not reported. Each item is deduplicated per meeting, actor, activity and Chicago day (`queueMentorMeetingActivityDigest`).
- **Notification setting:** M4 and M5 use the Mentor-only category **"Meetings and events"** (`mentorEvents`), on by default, listed and toggleable only for Mentor accounts and Parents with Mentor or Travel Lead staff access (`categoriesForUser`). They keep the 5 PM digest (one summary push). The Staff setting no longer affects them; Staff to-dos stay under Staff. `getMyPreferences.includesMentorMeetings` is kept for compatibility and is always false.
- **Series:** a Mentor's own series never invites its creator. A Parent with Mentor staff access creates, reads and changes their own Mentor series like any Mentor. Generation runs as the creator: a Parent whose grant was removed (or a deactivated Mentor) can't generate, so the series goes to `needsReview`.
- **Losing Mentor access** (a Parent's staff access removed, or the account deactivated): read paths treat the account as no longer a Mentor at once: `listMeetingMentors` and `forEvent` leave them out, they get no M4/M5, their created meetings lose creator rights (no edit, roster or M5), and generation ends their series sign-ups. The cleanup (`removeLostMentorSignups`, also exposed as the internal `mentorMeetings.removeLostMentorAccess`) deletes their rows on upcoming events and ends their series sign-ups; `accounts.updateParentStaffAccess` (removing a Parent's grant) and `accounts.updateAdultAccess` (deactivating a Mentor or moving them to Coach) call it in the same transaction.
- **Helper:** `listMeetingMentors` in `convex/lib/mentorMeetings.ts` lists a meeting's Mentor user ids (invited, accepted, declined, self, and the creator of a Mentor meeting) with their status, for notifications; accounts that are no longer Mentors are left out.
