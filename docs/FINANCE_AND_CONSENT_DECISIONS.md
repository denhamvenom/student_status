# Confirmed Finance and Consent Decisions

Updated October 12, 2026. These are agreed requirements for upcoming development, not completed features. They supersede older planning notes where the rules differ.

## Event withdrawal and fees

Separate the main event/travel fee from extra fees, such as a lunch charge. When withdrawal is approved, automatically cancel the unpaid portion of extra fees; retain the original charge and its cancellation in the financial history rather than deleting it.

The Coach or Travel Lead will have four decisions:

1. Approve withdrawal and return the full eligible paid amount to available funds.
2. Approve withdrawal and retain the paid amount as non-refundable.
3. Approve withdrawal and return a chosen partial amount to available funds. The amount may be as large as the full eligible paid amount.
4. Deny withdrawal, leaving participation and finances unchanged.

The decision screen must show how much has already been paid, distinguish the fee categories, show the amount being returned and the amount retained, and prevent returning more than was paid and remains eligible. All decisions and resulting financial changes must be logged with the acting account, time, amounts, affected charges and explanation. Approved withdrawal releases the event/room place while preserving history.

A return here moves money within the student's account. It does not mean a check or cash was given to the family. Actual payouts remain a separate financial-area action restricted to authorized financial staff.

Confirmed fee treatment: unpaid main event/travel balances remain owed unless the approver explicitly cancels them. The approval screen must offer an explicit cancellation choice, defaulting to keeping that balance owed, and log any cancellation. Paid extra fees join the main payment in the amount eligible for full or partial return; only their unpaid portion is automatically cancelled.

## Automatic application of returned funds

Returned funds automatically apply to other outstanding charges in the assigned priority order. Any remainder stays in the student's available funds. For this return-and-reallocation operation, exclude charges belonging to the withdrawn event so they do not immediately take back the returned credit. Any retained unpaid main event/travel balance remains a separate outstanding obligation; returning a paid amount does not silently cancel that debt.

Preserve the money's original source throughout return and reallocation. For example, returning a fundraising allocation must not turn it into a personal-payment balance eligible for cash payout.

## Personal payments and fundraising credits

Track personal payments and fundraising credits as different sources. Fundraising credits may be:

- Applied to another student.
- Carried over.
- Moved into the team account.

Record transfers between students without creating or losing money. Record when funds leave a student's balance for the team account, with source, amount, actor, date and reason. The team's own bank balance, spending and accounting are outside this app's scope. A transfer record does not execute a bank transaction.

No cash/check payout of fundraising credits to a family has been authorized. Preserve that restriction unless the user explicitly changes it. The role allowed to transfer fundraising credit and any restrictions on eligible recipient students still need to be defined before enabling those actions.

## Permission form and medication choices

Use these organization names in the form: **Denham Springs High School** and **FIRST Team 8044, Denham Venom**.

The permission wording must state that signing permits minor first aid as appropriate. This is a content requirement; the Coach is the confirmed final approver, and the exact wording remains pending. It does not remove the separate medication choices or authorize a real signature before the full consent workflow is approved.

Confirmed medication categories:

- Pain Medication.
- Allergy Medication.
- Cold Medication.

Each category offers **Allow**, **Contact me first**, or **Do not allow**, with nothing preselected. The products/ingredients covered, exceptions and administration policy remain to be finalized and approved by the Coach before real signatures are enabled.

## Retention

Retain archived records for five years starting when the Coach archives the student's account. This resolves the start date. Deletion coverage, backups, downloaded exports, holds and treatment of later reactivation still need to be defined before automatic deletion is built.

## Parallel development boundaries

Work can proceed on independent interfaces, draft forms and tests while the remaining policies are clarified. Changes to shared financial calculations, schema and event/room withdrawal behavior need an agreed contract and a single integration owner. Use Sol agents for implementation, Astra for major coordination/review, and verified GitHub checkpoints. Do not treat this decision record as production-deployment approval.
