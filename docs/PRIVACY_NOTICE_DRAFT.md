# Denham Venom Student Status and Timeclock — notice for owner review

Draft only. Do not publish until the organization approves the contact address, operational retention details and description of actual practices. This is product disclosure preparation, not a determination of legal compliance.

## Proposed public text

Denham Venom uses Student Status and Timeclock to coordinate team enrollment, student and parent setup, attendance, event readiness, permission forms and team financial participation.

Google sign-in provides account identity information such as your name, email address and profile image. Depending on your role and the information you submit, the apps also hold student and parent profiles, contact and emergency information, health information, attendance, participation and financial records, and permission-form evidence. These records support team administration and event preparation.

Access depends on your approved team role and family relationships. Signing in with Google does not by itself grant access to team records. The Coach approves enrollment and manages application roles. Shared Timeclock devices should use the designated Mentor account and must be signed out when no longer in authorized use.

The apps use Google for sign-in and the approved team calendar, Convex for application processing and storage, and Cloudflare for hosting. Timeclock reads the approved team calendar through a separate read-only service account; signing into the apps does not authorize access to your personal Google calendar.

The team’s agreed retention period is five years from Coach archival of an account. Detailed handling of linked records, exports and holds must be confirmed by the organization before this notice is published. Automatic deletion is not enabled. Do not claim automated backups or proven restoration in this notice while those remain deferred.

For questions about your information or to request a correction or review of access or retention, contact admin@team8044.org.
