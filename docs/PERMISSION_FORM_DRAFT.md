# Permission and medication form — Coach review draft

**Status: content draft only.** This is proposed language for the Coach's review, not an active form or authorization to travel, administer medication, or collect a signature. The application must keep permission submission disabled until the Coach approves final wording, the medication procedure is confirmed with school health staff, and a versioned signing workflow is implemented and tested.

## Proposed parent-facing wording

### Student and events covered

Denham Springs High School — FIRST Team 8044, Denham Venom.

This form would identify the student by legal name and identify each event it covers by name, date, destination and travel arrangement. Approval for one set of listed events would not automatically cover events added later. A parent or legal guardian would review updated event details and provide a separate authorization when required.

### Participation and transportation

I give permission for the student named on the form to participate in the specifically listed Denham Venom events and related supervised team activities. I understand that event schedules and travel arrangements will be communicated by the team and may change. I will review those details and contact the team if my student's participation or transportation needs change. Transportation authority, driver eligibility, and any separate school travel forms remain subject to applicable school procedures.

### Emergency contact and minor first aid

I will keep the student's emergency contacts, allergy information, medication information, and other relevant health details current, and I will tell the team promptly about changes before an event. If I cannot be reached during an illness or injury, I authorize the supervising team adults to seek appropriate emergency care for the student. Signing this form would permit minor first aid as appropriate. The final form must identify the responsible adults and any school-required procedures; this draft does not authorize medication administration.

### Medication choices — separate decision for each category

For **each** category below, the final form would require one explicit choice: **Allow**, **Contact me first**, or **Do not allow**. Nothing is preselected. An Allow choice would apply only to the exact products and ingredients named in the approved form and only under the school-approved administration procedure. A Contact me first choice requires contact before administration; it is not standing permission. A Do not allow choice records no permission for that category. Individual allergies, contraindications and exceptions must be reviewed separately.

| Category | Choices required on approved form |
| --- | --- |
| Pain Medication | Allow / Contact me first / Do not allow |
| Allergy Medication | Allow / Contact me first / Do not allow |
| Cold Medication | Allow / Contact me first / Do not allow |

The [Livingston Parish Public Schools medication policy JGCD](https://www.lpsb.org/UserFiles/Servers/Server_546/File/Policy/Policy%2024-25/October/JGCD-24%20Administration%20of%20Medication.pdf) describes prescription and nonprescription medication procedures, including a prescriber order and parent authorization. These category choices do not replace those procedures. Before approving final wording, the Coach must confirm with school health staff how the policy applies to team travel and who may administer any medication.

### Conduct and fees

The student is expected to follow team, school, event and adult-supervision instructions, including travel and conduct requirements. Event-specific fees and any extra charges should be shown with the event before a family commits. If a student asks to withdraw, the Coach or Travel Lead will decide whether to approve or deny the request. On approval, the decision can return all or part of eligible paid amounts to the student's available account funds or retain paid amounts as non-refundable. Returned funds may apply to other outstanding charges. Unpaid extra-fee portions are cancelled; an unpaid main event/travel balance remains owed unless the approver explicitly cancels it. A return to account funds is not a cash or check refund. The decision and its financial effects are recorded in the student's financial history.

### Future acknowledgment

The final workflow should show the precise wording version, covered event list, health details at signing, each medication choice, and the parent/legal guardian's verified account identity. It should require a separate affirmative acknowledgment and typed signature matching that account. A student acknowledgment, if required, and correction/re-sign rules remain to be settled. This draft contains no signature field because signing is disabled.

## Coach approval items before activation

- Approve exact participation, transport, emergency care, minor first aid, conduct and fee wording; confirm event scope and whether student acknowledgment is required.
- Confirm with school health staff how JGCD applies to team travel; specify products and ingredients, exceptions, prescriber orders, parent authorization, storage and responsible administration for each medication category.
- Define the final versioned form, event and health snapshot, correction and re-sign lifecycle, and any paper-form acceptance process. Test role and student-scope enforcement before enabling submissions.
